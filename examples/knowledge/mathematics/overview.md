# Why the maximum is c

The two assumed inequalities put a no higher than c. That makes c the maximum
of the pair. The argument does not give a strict inequality: a and c may be equal.

The [argument record](knowledge.scir) preserves the domain, each assumption,
the two conclusions, and the limitation they leave open. Its dependency graph
helps target review of conclusions and limitations when an assumption changes;
it is not itself a machine-checked proof.
